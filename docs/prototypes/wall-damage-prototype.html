Walls by segment

How it works

  • Each wall is cut into segments, one per wall unit. A 2-unit wall has 2 segments.
  • Each segment has its own health (3). A hit damages only the segment it lands on, and the cracks start where it landed.
  • A broken segment shatters and leaves a gap the ball can pass through. The rest of the wall stays up, with jagged ends.
