Shot Safe / Power

Try it

  • Press on the ball and drag back: the shot goes the other way.
  • Safe: big ring. Power grows as you pull out toward the limit.
  • Hold still on the ball for 1 s: the ring shrinks to Power. Power is strongest near the ball and fades toward the limit.
  • Let go back inside the small inner circle to cancel.
